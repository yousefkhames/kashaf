I built Kashaf, a free browser lab where you repair virtual hospital devices.

You get a service ticket: "the infusion pump alarms for a blockage but the line is clear". You unplug and lock out the device, inspect parts, measure test points and decide what to replace. Every wasted step costs points. Opening a live device costs more.

Four cases so far: infusion pump, patient monitor, defibrillator, suction pump. English and Arabic. Training only, fictional values.
